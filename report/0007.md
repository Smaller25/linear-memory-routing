# 0007 — Size scaling: SSC's net win grows with model size (2026-06-17)

Does the mamba2-1.3b result (0005 — trained low-rank SSC router on a *frozen* backbone beats vanilla
at the length where the fixed state collapses) hold at other sizes? Tested mamba2-130m and -2.7b on
the A100. The converter generalizes across sizes (reads `d_model`/`n_layer`; logit-match gate per
size). All routers use the **low-rank** option (r=64) — full routers are 805M @1.3b / ~1.7B @2.7b,
the low-rank ones ~31M / ~52M (≈25× smaller) and still win.

## mamba2-2.7b — SSC (low-rank 64, trained @train_len 2048 / 8 segments, bf16)
Held-out natural-language passkey, chunk 256, memory-light scorer:

| length | vanilla | +RM (training-free) | **+SSC** | Δ(SSC − vanilla) | segments |
|--------|--------|---------------------|----------|------------------|----------|
| 2048 | 0.986 | 0.891 | 0.978 | −0.007 | 8 |
| 4096 | 0.942 | 0.108 | 0.978 | +0.036 | 16 |
| **8192** | **0.500** | 0.000 | **1.000** | **+0.500** | 32 |

vanilla-2.7b collapses to 0.50 at 8192; **SSC reaches 1.000 (+0.50)** — a *larger* net win than 1.3b.

## Cross-size summary (SSC vs vanilla at 8192, the collapse length)
| model | vanilla@8192 | +SSC@8192 | net win | (full→low-rank router params) |
|-------|--------------|-----------|---------|-------------------------------|
| mamba2-1.3b (0005) | 0.738 | 0.986 | **+0.248** | 805M → 31M |
| **mamba2-2.7b** | 0.500 | 1.000 | **+0.500** | ~1.7B → 52M |

**The net win grows with model size**: the larger fixed-state model collapses harder at long context
(0.738 → 0.500), and SSC's cached-memory routing recovers it more completely (0.986 → 1.000). RM
(training-free) collapses at both sizes (8192: 0.00) — the trained router is essential.

## mamba2-130m — not a clean testbed (reported honestly)
130m is too small to show the structure: vanilla passkey is weak and ~flat (512: 0.83, 2048: 0.77,
8192: 0.61) — no "solves-short, collapses-long" regime, so there is no clean headroom for a net-win
demonstration. Also its converter logit-match was loose (max|Δ|≈0.12 vs 1.3b's 7e-3 / 2.7b's 1.8e-2)
— a likely 130m-specific config detail (headdim/d_state/ngroups) worth a closer look before trusting
130m numbers. Skipped the 130m SSC sweep; 1.3b and 2.7b are the informative sizes.

## Takeaway
The core result — **a small trained (low-rank) router over cached segment states lets a frozen
pretrained linear model exceed its fixed-state recall, exactly where the fixed state fails** — is not
a 1.3b artifact: it **scales up to 2.7b and gets stronger** (+0.50 net win at 8k). Low-rank routers
(~25× fewer params) are sufficient. Next: the GDN backbone (router training now routed through
`fused_recurrent` to dodge the chunk-backward A100-OOM / Hopper-bug), and AoM/MoM heads.
