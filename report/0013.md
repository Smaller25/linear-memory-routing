# 0013 — Learned segment-length distribution: the head segments per fact (2026-06-22)

Follow-up to 0012. The learned boundary head (a `Linear(d,1)` distilled from oracle positions)
recovered oracle-level multi-key recall up to kv 512 (0012). This report opens the box: **where does
it actually place boundaries?** We dump the predicted boundary mask at eval and measure the segment
lengths it induces (`train_mqar.py --dump-boundaries`; job 1242, from-scratch GDN-2 d256/4L,
train-kv 4–128, eval n=64/kv). Interactive figure: artifact `seg-length-dist`.

## Setup recap (unified MQAR protocol, this session)
From-scratch GDN-2 (d_model 256, 4 layers, ~6M), lr 3e-3, train-kv {4,8,16,32,64,128}, eval kv
{64,128,256,512}. Recall (accuracy at answer positions):

| model | kv64 | kv128 | kv256 | kv512 |
|-------|-----|------|------|------|
| vanilla (easy curriculum, saturates) | 0.92 | 0.32 | 0.03 | 0.002 |
| oracle (per-fact boundaries)         | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| **learned** boundaries               | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.999 |

(Vanilla trained on the full kv 4–128 mix does NOT converge — it can't fit the hard-kv examples; the
saturation row above is vanilla trained on an easy curriculum it *can* learn, kv 4–32. The method
trains on the full mix and converges.)

## Segment-length distribution (the finding)
| kv | seq len T | seg / row | median len | mean len | len-2 share | query-region seg len |
|----|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|-------------|----------------------|
| 64  | 256  | 66.3  | 2 | 3.9 | 98.5% | 128 |
| 128 | 512  | 130.2 | 2 | 3.9 | 99.2% | 256 |
| 256 | 1024 | 258.2 | 2 | 4.0 | 99.6% | 512 |
| 512 | 2048 | 514.1 | 2 | 4.0 | 99.8% | 1024 |

- **The median learned segment is length 2 at every kv.** The MQAR context is laid out `[k,v,k,v,…]`,
  so a length-2 segment is exactly one `(key,value)` fact. The head learned *what a fact boundary is*,
  not a fixed stride.
- **The count scales with #facts**: ~66/130/258/514 segments per sequence ≈ kv+2, including kv 256/512
  that were never in the training curriculum (≤128). This is why recall generalizes past the training
  range — the segmentation does.
- **One segment per sequence is the exception**: the long post-context query region (length ≈ T/2).
  That single long segment is the entire reason mean (~4) sits above median (2).

## Read-out
The learned boundaries are not "every C tokens" — they are per-fact, matching the oracle that drove
the 0012 win, and they track the fact count as it grows. The earlier Phase-1 failure (0012) was a
narrow curriculum teaching a *position* bias (~8 boundaries regardless of kv); the wide curriculum
fixes it into a *content* rule ("a fact just ended → cut"), which is what this distribution shows.

## Caveats
- Boundaries are still learned WITH oracle supervision (distillation). Unsupervised boundary learning
  (task-loss / budget penalty) remains open (0012 milestone 1).
- Read-out summary here is the pooled-hidden proxy; true-state read-out (0012) is validated separately.
