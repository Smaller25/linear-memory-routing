# 0014 — RULER free-generation, frozen mamba2-370m (2026-06-22, +SSC pending)

The publication-grade RULER protocol (greedy free generation + official string-match), on a frozen
**mamba2-370m** with a passkey-trained **SSC** read-out in the decode loop (`predict_ruler.py`,
report 0012). This is the frozen-retrofit track at a small scale, on a real-text benchmark — the
setting the user expected to be weak. Status: **vanilla baseline measured; +SSC running (job 1264)**.

## Setup
- Backbone: `state-spaces/mamba2-370m` (≈360M) → FLA, bf16, frozen. SSC router: k=4, low-rank 64,
  chunk 256, passkey-trained (`ckpt/ssc_370m.pt`).
- Eval: NVIDIA RULER (vendored generators), tasks `niah_single_1`, `niah_multikey_2`. Greedy
  free-gen (128 tokens), **official `string_match_all`** via `scripts/ruler.py eval`.

## Result — vanilla vs +SSC, matched samples, official metric
Matched n=30 (same RULER instances for both arms; job 1264):

| length | task | vanilla | **+SSC** |
|--------|------|---------|----------|
| **2048** | niah_single_1   | 0.00 | **36.67** |
| **2048** | niah_multikey_2 | 0.00 | 3.33 |
| 4096 | niah_single_1   | 0.00 | (SSC timed out) |
| 4096 | niah_multikey_2 | 0.00 | (SSC timed out) |

**+SSC gives a real net win on the standard free-gen protocol**: frozen mamba2-370m floors (0.00) on
single-needle RULER at 2048, and the passkey-trained SSC read-out lifts it to **36.7** (zero-shot —
trained on passkey, not on RULER). Multi-key stays near-floor (3.3) — consistent with 0010 (SSC is
single-needle, not interference). So the frozen-retrofit thesis transfers to real text + free
generation, at least at 2048 on single-needle.

mamba2-370m is OOD past its ~2k pretraining; on a *different* (n=32) sample set vanilla niah_single
@4k scored 28.1 (job 1257) but 0.00 on the n=30 set here — i.e., near-floor with high small-n variance,
so only the matched comparison above is trustworthy.

## Process notes (hard-won; don't re-discover)
- **RULER generators need extra deps** beyond the repo: `nltk` (+punkt), `wonderwords`, `tenacity`.
  Without them `prepare` silently produces no jsonl (`cmd_prepare` swallows the subprocess stdout
  error). Added to `setup_env.sh`.
- **`predict_ruler.py`'s INLINE metric is buggy** — it reported 0.00 even when the gold answer was
  present in the generation (e.g., pred "…is: 5437923", gold 5437923). The official
  `scripts/ruler.py eval` on the same `pred.jsonl` gives the correct 28.1. **Use the official eval for
  all numbers**; fix the inline metric later.
- **Free-gen + read-out is slow at 8k** (the decode loop re-runs cached segments per token): the full
  4k+8k × 2-task × 2-variant × 50-sample run hit the 4h Slurm limit after the vanilla pass. Hence the
  2048/4096, n=30 matched compare (job 1264).
- Disk on the shared root volume runs ~95% full; clearing pip/triton caches unblocked earlier OOD
  failures (see `greenbeard-cluster` note: prefer /data2).

## The operational bottleneck
+SSC at 4096 (and 8192) **timed out at the 4h Slurm limit** — twice. Free generation with the read-out
in the decode loop re-runs the cached prompt segments at every generated token, which is too slow past
2k for 30–50 samples × 2 tasks. Getting 4k/8k +SSC numbers needs the batched / intermediate-state
read-out kernel (0012 milestone 3), or a teacher-forced approximation (faster, but not the official
free-gen protocol; cf. 0009's caveat).

## Verdict & caveat
On real-text RULER free generation, the frozen mamba2-370m + zero-shot SSC retrofit gives a clear net
win on single-needle at 2048 (0.00 → 36.7, matched samples); multi-key stays near-floor (0010). It is
weak/OOD past ~2k, and +SSC free-gen does not yet scale to 4k/8k in the time budget. Small model, small
n, single-answer tasks — this characterizes the *frozen* track honestly; the headline is the
from-scratch Track B (0012/0013/0015).
